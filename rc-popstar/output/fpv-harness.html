fpv-harness

Diagram

Powers the 5.8GHz analog FPV system (RunCam RC Cadet AIO or equivalent) from the XD10 Pro ESC BEC via a Pololu D24V5F5 5V step-down regulator. BEC is adjustable 5–7.4V; the regulator provides a stable 5V regardless of BEC setting. Skip the regulator only if BEC is confirmed at 5.0V. The FPV link is independent of the RC control system — no radio channels used. See docs/fpv.md for mounting, channel map, and VTX disable procedure.
