gyro-dbox3-pro

Diagram

Channel map for the D-Box3 Pro gyro. Adds PCA (Priority Control Adjustment) and AGS (Anti-Glitch System) onboard dials vs the Lite version. Remote gain via CH4 works on both Lite and Pro.
Verified from manual: hpiracing.com/assets/documents/manuals/d_box3_gyro/161068_006_sg_dbox3_gyro_en.pdf
Receiver connections (from manual verbatim):
1. Connect the 4-pin adapter cable to the gyro.
2. Connect the 3-pin steering lead to receiver CH1 (Steering).
3. Connect the single-wire lead to CH4 (or another AUX channel for Gain Adjustment).

Channel map with this gyro:
CH1 (steering wheel) → D-Box3 Pro CH1 in + Killerbody 48102 CH1 (Y-cable)
CH2 (throttle) → XD10 Pro ESC + Killerbody 48102 CH2 (Y-cable)
CH3 (VR knob) → FREE — R7FG built-in gyro MUST be OFF (factory default)
Do NOT assign CH3 to R7FG gyro sensitivity when external
gyro is connected.
CH4 (SwA momentary) → D-Box3 Pro GAIN (single-wire, optional)
CH5 (SwB 3-way) → FREE
CH6/PPM (SwC) → FREE
CH7/SBUS (SwD) → FREE

D-Box3 Pro extras vs Lite:
PCA dial — balance between driver steering and gyro correction (CW = more driver)
AGS dial — speed of steering return to center (CW = faster return)
Lite: GAIN dial only, no PCA or AGS.

R7FG built-in gyro conflict (from R7FG manual, radiolink.com/rc6gsv3_manual):
Factory default = OFF. Enable by pressing binding button 3 times.
If enabled while D-Box3 Pro is also connected, both gyros correct CH1 simultaneously.
Leave R7FG gyro OFF when using the D-Box3 Pro.

Specs (from vestergaard product page, vestergaardgroup.com):
Input voltage: 4.8V–10V, 20.0 × 15.5 × 8.8mm, 5.4g
